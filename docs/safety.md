# Safety case and release gates

This repository is a research model. It is not a certified rideable bicycle.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Do not print a full-scale steering column, axle, brake mount, hub shell, or load-bearing frame from unqualified hobby filament and ride it.
- Do not encapsulate lithium cells inside a structural print.
- Do not use improvised aramid rope or fabric as the drive belt.
- Do not remove independent mechanical brakes because regenerative braking exists.
- Do not raise power or assistance speed without destination-specific legal and thermal review.
- Do not call the captive wheel theft-proof.

## Required gates

1. **Material characterization:** production-orientation tensile, compression, flexural, inter-layer, impact, creep, UV, water, salt, and thermal-cycle data with lower tolerance bounds.
2. **Component proof and fatigue:** monoblades, axle shoulders, retention covers, steering, bottom bracket, seat structure, wheels, brake mounts, battery and motor mounts.
3. **Drivetrain qualification:** traceable belt construction, pretension, alignment, tooth-jump/shear margin, environmental exposure, pulley wear, automatic-shift failure cases, guard and drainage.
4. **Tire and handling:** rolling resistance, heat, wet/dry grip, curb/pothole impact, rim retention, delamination, vibration and damaged-tire behavior.
5. **Electrical safety:** qualified pack, BMS, charger, fusing, isolation, ingress protection, propagation resistance, fault testing and emergency disconnect.
6. **System/regulatory review:** braking, steering, fatigue, electrical system, battery, EMC, lighting, speed/power classification, labeling, instructions and recall traceability.

A rideable release requires signed test reports, controlled drawings and tolerances, approved material/process specifications, bill of materials, supplier certificates, hazard analysis, FMEA/FTA, maintenance intervals, inspection limits, and serialized traceability. Repository CI checks software and CAD routing only.
