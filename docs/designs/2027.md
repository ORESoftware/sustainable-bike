# 2027 Design: Circular-Service Upgrade

The 2027 profile keeps the 2026 safety boundary while improving low-speed gearing, tire service, energy modularity, idealized range, disassembly time, and material circularity.

## Architecture changes

- One 58-tooth front ring and one 24-tooth rear belt sprocket.
- Wider 14 mm dry aramid/Kevlar-class synchronous belt.
- Three fully automatic internal rear ratios: 0.80, 1.00, and 1.62.
- No rider-operated shifter; torque and cadence logic coordinate internal shifts with motor torque reduction.
- Wider airless-lattice tire with a replaceable tread ring rather than disposing of the whole wheel structure.
- Two independently serviceable 288 Wh LFP modules, 576 Wh total.
- Recyclable continuous-fiber thermoplastic shells around replaceable metal safety inserts.
- Four-fastener-family target, 45-minute disassembly target, and 45% recycled-content target.

## Performance intent

The low ratio reduces the nominal 82 rpm road speed to roughly 12.5 mph for starts and small hills. The high ratio is approximately 25 mph at nominal cadence. Lower modeled rolling resistance, slightly reduced mass and drag, and higher system efficiency improve the idealized continuous power margin without increasing rated motor power.

## New qualification work

The airless lattice requires heat, debris, impact, fatigue, rolling-resistance, wet-grip, and tread-retention testing. The automatic three-speed unit requires shift endurance, torque-interruption, stuck-ratio, sensor disagreement, and fail-safe testing. The dual battery modules require isolation, mismatch, connector-temperature, ingress, interlock, and hot-plug prevention validation.
